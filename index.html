THE CONCEPT

How Will It Work?

The real integration will be built in stages to protect wallets and correctly verify blockchain transactions.

01

Confirmed Purchase

The server will detect an eligible and confirmed purchase of $WRACE.

02

Wallet Entry

The public wallet address will appear in shortened form as a racer.

03

Fast Race

Eligible wallets will participate in an animated race lasting approximately two minutes.

04

Verifiable Result

The winning wallet and reward transaction can be displayed publicly and verified on-chain.

FAIR PLAY

Proposed Rules

01

One Entry

Each eligible wallet receives one entry in every race round.

02

Minimum Purchase

The minimum eligible purchase amount will be configured and publicly announced.

03

Confirmation

Purchases must be successfully confirmed on the Solana blockchain.

04

Public Proof

The winning wallet and reward payment can be reviewed publicly on Solana.

🔒 Wallet Security

Wallet Racers will never request seed phrases, passwords or private keys. Real rewards should be sent from a separate prize wallet with a limited balance.
